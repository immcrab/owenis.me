Your project deserves a better address.

Firebase hands you a deploy URL with a hash in the middle of it. Owenis.me gives you a short one you can say out loud — and it keeps working when you redeploy, rename, or move the site somewhere else entirely.

Letters, numbers and dashes. Reserving opens an email to us.

One link that outlives the deploy it points at.

  • Point it anywhere

    A hosting site, a preview channel, a callable function, a doc on another domain. If it has a URL, a slug can reach it.

  • Repoint without reprinting

    Move /aurora from the staging channel to production and every link you already shared follows along.

  • Counts, not surveillance

    Clicks per slug, per day, and where they came from. No cookies, no profiles, nothing handed to anyone else.

Three steps, about a minute.

  1. Paste the Firebase URL

    The long one from your deploy output — aurora-notes-3f9c2a.web.app. Preview channel URLs work too.

  2. Pick the slug

    Whatever you'd actually type. Taken names tell you straight away instead of after you hit save.

  3. Share the short one

    Redirects go live immediately. Change the destination later and the slug doesn't move.

Something broken, or a slug you want held?

Write to us and a person reads it. Include the slug and the URL it should point at, and we'll usually have an answer the same day.
