Your login emails come from a stranger.

Firebase signs every verification and password-reset email as noreply@ your project id .firebaseapp.com. People don't recognise it, and neither do spam filters. Pick a name here and those same emails go out from owenis.me instead.

Read this before you start.

  • The sender changes

    Every auth email Firebase sends for your project — verification, password reset, email change, sign-in links — arrives from the name you pick.

  • Action URLs don't

    The links inside those emails still point at your own Firebase domain. Owenis.me doesn't host action URLs, and asking for one won't get you one.

  • We hold the DNS

    owenis.me is our zone, so you can't add the records Firebase asks you for. That's the whole reason this form exists — paste them and we'll add them.

Five steps. Most of them are Firebase's.

  1. Open your email templates

    Firebase console, then Authentication, then Templates. Any template will do — they all share one sender.

  2. Start customising the domain

    Click the pencil on the template, then the customise-domain option next to the From address. Enter owenis.me.

  3. Copy every record Firebase shows you

    You'll get a table of TXT, CNAME and MX rows. Copy all of it, host names and values included. Don't leave the page — you'll need it verified later.

  4. Paste it below

    Along with your project ID and the name you want in front of the @. That's everything we need.

  5. We add the records, you hit verify

    We'll email you once they're live in the zone — usually within a day. Go back to that Firebase page and click verify, and the sender switches over.
